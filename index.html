About us

About MONARCH

MONARCH is more than a barbershop. It's a place for men who value style, confidence and flawless quality in every detail.

Our barbers combine the classic traditions of the craft with modern technique, building a look that reveals your character instead of hiding it.

Low light, aged wood, cedar in the air and a glass of whiskey in your hand — we built a place worth returning to for more than the haircut.
